Execution Transparency

The Grihakon 5-Stage Project Journey

We operate with strict engineering milestones, ensuring total transparency, zero cost inflation, and guaranteed delivery within 45 days.

1
Stage 01 • Day 1 to 3

Laser Survey & Structural Diagnostic

Our site engineer visits your property in Baranagar, Dunlop, Bonhooghly, or elsewhere in Greater Kolkata. We capture millimeter-accurate digital laser measurements, audit structural wall dampness levels with moisture meters, examine existing beam configurations, and map water inlet/outlet points for modular kitchen optimization.

Deliverable: Comprehensive As-Built CAD Dimension Floor Plan & Civil Health Report.

2
Stage 02 • Day 4 to 10

3D Architectural VR Modeling & Material Selection

Our visualization studio develops ultra-realistic 3D walkthroughs of your living lounges, modular kitchens, and bedroom wardrobes. You visit our 96 Deshbandhu Road design studio to touch and select actual material samples: quartz countertops, anti-fingerprint acrylic swatches, natural teak veneers, and Hafele/Blum hardware hinges.

Deliverable: Signed Photorealistic 3D Renders, Material Specification Bill, and Locked Project Budget.

3
Stage 03 • Day 11 to 25

Factory Precision Fabrication (IS:710 Marine Plywood)

Cabinetry carcasses, modular drawer boxes, and sliding wardrobes are precision cut and edge-banded with zero glue bleed in our North Kolkata production facility. We strictly utilize 100% boiling-water-proof (BWP) IS:710 calibrated marine plywood with verifiable factory stamps.

Deliverable: Pre-assembled modular modules and factory quality check certification.

4
Stage 04 • Day 26 to 40

On-Site Installation, False Ceiling & Smart Lighting

Our master installation carpenters assemble cabinetry modules on site with laser-guided levels. Concurrently, our specialized electrical team runs concealed conduits, mounts Gyproc false ceiling channels, fits magnetic LED tracks, and installs marble/tile TV feature walls.

Deliverable: Bi-weekly photo & video progress updates sent directly to your WhatsApp.

5
Stage 05 • Day 41 to 45

Deep Cleaning, 50-Point Snag Clearance & Warranty Handover

Before handoff, our quality assurance supervisor performs a 50-point inspection verifying hinge alignment, soft-close dampers, light color temperature harmony, and paint perfection. Professional post-construction deep cleaning leaves your home immaculate and move-in ready.

Deliverable: Keys to your finished residence + Official Signed 10-Year Hardware Warranty Certificate.

Baranagar's distinguished interior architecture and residential renovation studio. 10+ years of uncompromised carpentry, photorealistic 3D visualization, and certified marine-grade craftsmanship.
