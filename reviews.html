Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers to Common Questions

How does Grihakon ensure the 45-day handover guarantee?

All cabinetry carcasses and drawer modules are pre-fabricated and edge-banded in our factory before arriving on site. On-site work focuses purely on laser-aligned assembly, electrical integration, and civil finishes, eliminating on-site carpentry delays.

How can I verify that my plywood is genuine IS:710 Marine Grade?

Every plywood sheet delivered to your site carries the certified BIS IS:710 boiling water proof stamp and QR code verification. You are invited to inspect all raw timber boards before laminates or veneers are applied.

What does your 10-Year Hardware Warranty cover?

Our warranty covers all Blum and Hafele mechanical hinges, drawer channels, hydraulic lift-ups, sliding wardrobe tracks, and structural carcass integrity against borer/termite degradation and delamination.
